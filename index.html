Transparent Demonstration Rule Set

Deterministic multi-factor risk logic. No black-box machine learning models.

DEMO LOGIC SPECIFICATION
RULE 1 (HIGHEST PRIORITY) REMOVE
Trigger Condition: Expiry date in past  OR  Tray Temperature ≥ 9.0°C

Product has reached or exceeded its expiration date, or the refrigerator has warmed to an unsafe threshold where rapid microbial growth occurs.

Action: Discard item immediately to prevent cross-contamination.
RULE 2 (HIGH PRIORITY) SELL FIRST
Trigger Condition: Expiry date is today  OR  Expiry within next 2 calendar days

Applied only when Rule 1 conditions do not apply. Product remains safe under current cooling but shelf life is immediately ending.

Action: Mark down, prioritize for immediate consumption, or place on front counter.
RULE 3 (MODERATE PRIORITY) INSPECT
Trigger Condition: Tray Temp > 5.0°C and < 9.0°C  OR  Expiry within next 4 calendar days

Applied when higher priority rules do not apply. Temperature is above optimal 5.0°C preservation limit or product shelf life is moderately approaching.

Action: Inspect package integrity, verify chiller cooling seals, and monitor status.
RULE 4 (DEFAULT) SAFE
Trigger Condition: Tray Temp ≤ 5.0°C  AND  Product Expiry > 4 calendar days

Cold chain is strictly preserved and product is well within manufacturer longevity guidelines.

Action: Maintain standard chilled storage.
Important Demonstration Notice: These classification rules are configurable software thresholds designed for system integration demonstration and do not constitute certified laboratory food-safety guarantees. Atmospheric MQ135 gas readings generate separate environmental alerts and do not independently alter product risk status.
